Chase Construction LLC builds and restores custom log homes and big timber frame homes in Sun Valley, Ketchum, Hailey and Bellevue, Idaho. Founded by Mike Chase, our crew covers the full life of a log home: new custom log home construction, timber framing and trusses, log home restoration and rot repair, chinking and re-chinking, staining and sealing, media blasting, log siding and custom log finishes, and traditional framing.
