On-Site Milling,
Done Right

Chase Construction operates on-site bandsaw and circular milling equipment — a capability that most residential builders don't have and can't replicate. We produce lumber cut to specification for our own builds, for contractors who need dimensional material outside standard stock, and for specialty applications that simply don't exist in any catalog.

Having milling capability in-house changes what's possible on a build. When a log profile doesn't exist in standard dimensions, we cut it. When a structural element requires a specific section, we produce it.

Common Questions

About our milling
& specialty services.

What wood species do you mill?+

We work primarily with Douglas fir, ponderosa pine, lodgepole pine, and larch — species common to the Idaho region and well-suited to both structural and finish applications. If you have a specific species requirement, ask us and we'll tell you if it's achievable.

Can you produce custom cut lumber for a project you're not building?+

Yes. We mill for other contractors, builders, and individual clients. You don't need to be a Chase construction client to use our milling services. Contact us with your dimensions and quantity and we'll give you a straight answer on lead time and cost.

What's the minimum order for custom milling?+

We evaluate requests based on the material and scope involved. Small specialty orders are often workable — especially for unique profiles or species that aren't available elsewhere. Reach out with your specs.
