From Vision
to Foundation

A Chase custom log home begins with a conversation, not a catalog. We work with clients who know what they want to feel when they walk through the door — and need a builder who can translate that into structure, material, and form.

Our homes are designed for the Sun Valley environment: the snowpack, the altitude, the sightlines, the way light moves through a mountain morning. Everything else follows from there.

Design Consultation

We don't hand you a blueprint. We sit with you — often remotely — and walk through exactly what you're imagining. We ask the questions that surface the things you haven't thought of yet.

Scope and Budget

Construction

Completion

The Craft Behind Every Log

Log construction is not framing
with bigger lumber.

It requires a different knowledge, a different set of hands, and a different approach to every joint, every corner, every load point.

Material Selection

We source logs for their structural character and visual presence. Every build is a collaboration between what the client envisions and what the material can honestly do.

Joinery and Assembly

The way logs are joined, stacked, and settled determines how the home performs over decades. Our crew has done this long enough to know what holds and what doesn't.

Longevity by Design

A custom log home built by Chase is not a ten-year house. It is a multi-generational structure — designed and built to outlast the first family that moves into it.

Common Questions

What clients ask
before they build.

How long does a custom log home take to build?+

Most builds in our range run 12–24 months from groundbreaking to completion, depending on scope, site access, and permitting. We give you a realistic timeline in the scope phase — not an optimistic one.

Can I be involved in design decisions if I'm not in Idaho?+

Yes — and most of our clients aren't. We use video calls, shared documentation, and regular photo updates to bring you into every decision that matters. You won't feel absent from your own build.

What's included in full-service construction management?+

Everything between your design approval and your walkthrough: trade coordination, permitting, material procurement, site supervision, vendor management, inspections, and ongoing communication. You decide. We execute.
