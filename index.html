Before the Snow Flies

Cracked chinking won't
survive an Idaho winter.

The pale lines between your logs are the only thing keeping snowmelt out of the wood. When chinking cracks or pulls away — or the stain goes gray and chalky — water gets behind it, and freeze-thaw does the rest. We re-chink and re-stain log homes across Sun Valley, Ketchum, and Hailey, and late summer is the right time to get on the schedule.

  • Cracks along the log line
  • Chinking pulling away
  • Stain gone gray or chalky

First Conversation

Common Questions

What clients ask
before they reach out

Do you work with clients who are still early in planning? +

Yes. Many clients come to us before they have an architect or finalized plans. Getting us involved early often prevents costly design decisions that don't translate well to log construction. The first conversation is free and carries no obligation.

How many projects does Chase take on each year? +

We limit our project load deliberately. This is not a high-volume company. When you build with Chase, you get our full attention — the whole team's — and that requires keeping the number of active projects manageable. We recommend reaching out early if you have a target timeline.

Can I be involved in design decisions if I'm not in Idaho? +

Yes — and most of our clients aren't. We use video calls, shared documentation, and regular photo updates to bring you into every decision that matters. You won't feel absent from your own build. Managing remote clients is not an accommodation for us — it's the standard.
