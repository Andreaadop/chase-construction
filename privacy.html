Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 17, 2026  ·  Last updated: July 17, 2026

Chase Construction LLC (“Chase Construction,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website at www.chaseconstruction.org and to our official social media pages, including Facebook and Instagram (together, our “Services”).

By using our Services or contacting us, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Services.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us

When you fill out a form on our website (such as a contact request or a free home-inspection request), message us on social media, or call us, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Details about your project, property, or inquiry, and any other information you choose to share

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, certain information is collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies, including:

  • Your device type, browser, and operating system
  • Your approximate location and IP address
  • Pages you view, links you click, and how you arrived at our site
  • Date and time of your visit

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide quotes, consultations, and services
  • Schedule and follow up on home inspections and project conversations
  • Communicate with you about your project, including by phone, email, or text if you have contacted us
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website and content
  • Understand how visitors use our site through analytics
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies, including Google Tag Manager and analytics tools such as Google Analytics, to understand site traffic and improve your experience. We may also use tools from social media platforms such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram) to measure the performance of our content and ads.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect how some parts of the site function. To opt out of Google Analytics, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to run our business, including with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our Services — for example, our website host, our customer-relationship and form-processing platform (GoHighLevel), and analytics providers such as Google.
  • Social media platforms when you interact with us there, subject to their own privacy policies.
  • Legal and safety reasons — when required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Chase Construction, our clients, or others.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, sale, or transfer of our business assets.

5. Third-Party Links & Social Media

Our Services may link to third-party websites or platforms we do not control, including Facebook, Instagram, and Google. This policy does not cover their practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party site or platform you use.

6. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, provide our services, and meet legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. When it is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices & Rights

You may:

  • Ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you
  • Opt out of marketing messages at any time — reply STOP to texts, or contact us using the details below
  • Control cookies through your browser settings

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or similar state privacy laws. To make a request, contact us using the information below and we will respond as required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

9. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a “Do Not Track” signal. Because there is no common industry standard for how to respond, our website does not currently respond to these signals.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our Services are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will be posted on this page. Your continued use of our Services after an update means you accept the revised policy.
