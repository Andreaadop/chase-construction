The Company

Built on judgment.
Experience. Knowledge.

Chase Construction was built on the belief that construction — real, complex, large-scale construction — requires more than tools and crew. It requires judgment. Experience. The kind of knowledge that only comes from doing something difficult many times and learning from every single one of them.

Mike Chase has spent his career building that knowledge in the Sun Valley area, and it shows in the homes, the restorations, and the relationships that carry his name.

Meet the Builder

Mike Chase

Mike has been building in the Sun Valley area for over two decades. In that time, he has led the majority of significant log home projects in the region — a fact that is not a marketing claim, but a reflection of how specialized this work is and how few builders have the depth to do it well.

He started in the field, not behind a desk. He knows what a log home requires because he has built them — every phase, every trade, every problem that comes from building large structures in a mountain climate with clients who expect the best.

He leads every Chase project personally. When you hire Chase Construction, you are hiring Mike — not a company that happens to employ someone named Mike.

What Mike Believes About Building

That details accumulate into character. That a client's trust is earned through consistency, not promises. That the best measure of a build is how it holds up twenty years after the final nail.

What Mike Believes About Clients

That they deserve a straight answer. That they shouldn't be managing a construction project — that's what they're paying us for. That the experience of building with Chase should be as considered as the home that results from it.

What We Stand For

What We Believe In
