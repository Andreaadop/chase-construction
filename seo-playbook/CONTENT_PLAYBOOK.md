# Content playbook

What to publish, how to structure it, and when. Distilled from the source
playbook + what actually moved the needle on mainpups.

## Pick clusters, not keywords

Research first (exact volumes, low competition), then commit to a **cluster**:
one hub page targeting the broad term + many spoke pages targeting the
specific ones. Example that worked: hub "dog breed personalities" + a spoke
per breed. The hub links every spoke; every spoke links the hub with
exact-anchor text; spokes cross-link where natural. Google reads the
structure as topical authority — on mainpups the hub was the first page to
reach striking distance, within weeks, on a brand-new domain.

Formats that rank, per the source playbook: "best X", "X vs Y",
"X alternatives", "how to X", and listicle gift/resource guides.

## Cadence: one quality post per week

One genuinely good post per week beats seven mediocre ones. "Good" means:
matches the format already ranking for the query, is better than the current
top 5, and contains something original (your data, your photos, your tested
experience — the thing AI-generated competitor content can't fake).

Sameday repurposing (each post, pick at least one):
- X thread / LinkedIn post with the keyword in the first line, link back.
- Reddit answer where the question is actually being asked, link where allowed.
- These platforms index in hours and are your first backlinks on a new domain.

## Seasonal content: publish 8–10 weeks early

New pages need 6–10 weeks to settle into their real rankings. Christmas/BFCM
content ships mid-September, not November. Valentine's ships early December.
If you're choosing between "perfect later" and "live now" for seasonal pages,
live now wins — you can improve a page that's already aging into position.

## Commercial listicles: be honestly biased

Gift guides / "best X" posts featuring your own products work IF:
- You disclose ("some of these are ours — marked").
- You include genuinely good items you don't sell.
- Every claim about your own product is true.
Pure self-promo listicles rank worse and convert worse. The honest version
also becomes pitchable to real bloggers later (see backlinks).

## Backlinks (the slow loop, run monthly not weekly)

1. Find pages already ranking for your commercial keywords ("best gifts for
   X" roundups etc.) and pitch inclusion — a sample or a small fee is often
   enough. You get the click-through traffic AND the authority.
2. Ask ChatGPT/Perplexity/Claude your money questions ("best X for Y") — note
   which sources they cite, pitch those sources.
3. Convert unlinked brand mentions into links once you have any mentions.
4. Cross-link your own sites where topically honest.

## Per-post checklist

- [ ] Target query in: title tag (first 60 chars), H1, one H2, URL slug.
- [ ] Meta description ≤155 chars with the query + a reason to click.
- [ ] 2+ internal links IN (from existing pages) and 2+ OUT (to your hub /
      related pages), descriptive anchors.
- [ ] Added to its hub page's list, if it's a spoke.
- [ ] Original element included (photo, data, tested detail).
- [ ] Cover image with alt text. (Free stock: Pexels API — key is free;
      requests need a User-Agent header or you get 403s.)
- [ ] Request indexing (GSC + Bing) after publishing.
- [ ] Repurposed to one social platform same day.
