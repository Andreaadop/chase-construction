## Naming note (from Search Console + volume data)

"Log **cabin**" out-searches "log **home**" on informational terms
(chinking: 3,600 vs 1,000 · maintenance: 590 vs 480 · restoration: 320-LOW vs
480-MED). Keep "log home" on service pages (existing decision — it's what
converts locally), but blog posts should target the cabin variant where volume
says so. One post can carry both ("log cabin chinking (also called log home
chinking)").
